Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Overview

SCS Play ("the App") is developed by Messkraft. This policy describes how we handle your data.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The current Google Play release stores game progress and preferences, such as scores, achievements, settings, and gameplay or feature-usage history, locally in the App's storage on your device. Messkraft does not receive this local gameplay data. If you choose to share a result, the content you select is passed to the app or service you choose through Android's share interface.

3. Accounts and Cloud Sync

The current Google Play release does not offer account creation, sign-in, or cloud sync. It runs in guest mode, so no account identifier or email address is collected by Messkraft.

4. Third-Party Services

The App loads typefaces from Google Fonts. When this resource is requested, Google may process technical request data such as your IP address, device or browser information, and request time.

  • Google Fonts - provides the App's typefaces
  • Android backup and restore - may back up or restore local App data if device backup is enabled

Android backup settings are controlled by you and your device provider; Messkraft does not access those backups. Google's handling of Fonts requests and Google-managed device backups is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising

The current Google Play release contains no advertising and does not include a third-party advertising SDK.

6. Analytics and Purchases

The App creates an engagement report from gameplay and feature-usage signals stored locally on your device. It does not send this report to Messkraft or to an analytics service. The current Google Play release does not offer real-money in-app purchases and does not include a billing SDK. Any in-game currency is virtual, is earned or used only within the App, and has no cash value.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete the App's local data by clearing its storage in Android settings. Uninstalling normally removes on-device App data, but a device backup may retain or later restore a copy; use your Android or Google backup controls to remove that copy. Because the current release has no accounts or cloud sync, Messkraft does not retain an account or cloud save to delete.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries: privacy@messkraft.com
